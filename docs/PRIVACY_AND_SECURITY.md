# Privacy and publication policy

This repository is intended to be **public**. Review every proposed commit accordingly.

## Never publish
- Personal names tied to student registration, personal contact information, phone numbers, email addresses, student IDs or similar identifiers.
- Original team roster documents, institutional coordination sheets with contact details, intake forms, or screenshots of private chats.
- Interview recordings, full transcripts, personal quotes, participant identifiers, or survey exports without explicit informed permission.
- Access tokens, passwords, environment files, service account keys, or private API credentials.
- Real student documents, immigration/visa records, or other sensitive materials.

## Allowed with review
- General product idea, architecture proposals, anonymous process descriptions.
- Publicly available institutional source links with accurate citations.
- Aggregate, truly anonymized research findings where consent and publication terms permit.
- Mock user-interface screenshots with fictional names and sanitized content.

## Before publishing
1. Inspect file content and metadata, including DOCX/PDF attachments and image EXIF.
2. Confirm interviews and screenshots have publication permission.
3. Verify that example data is artificial.
4. Check the Git history for accidental leaks: deleting a file later does not necessarily remove it from history.
5. Store private working documents outside this repository.

## App-level privacy (future)
Aim for data minimization, no unnecessary user accounts, no sensitive document collection, limited analytics, clear consent and retention practices, and source-linked advice. Treat AI outputs as informational, not official guidance.
