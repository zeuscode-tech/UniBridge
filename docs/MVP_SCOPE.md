# Proposed MVP scope

## Core users
New international students looking for trusted, understandable orientation information.

## First release (proposed)

### Must have
- University / language choice (only content the team can maintain).
- Home screen with clear next actions.
- First-weeks checklist with source and last-reviewed date.
- Essential information categories and article views.
- Search or simple FAQ over curated content.
- Prominent disclaimer that instructions must be verified with official institutions.

### Nice to have
- Multiple supported universities.
- Offline caching of non-sensitive guides.
- Simple user feedback: "Was this helpful?"
- User-adjustable text sizing and accessible UI labels.

### Stretch goals
- AI Q&A grounded in vetted university sources.
- Multilingual AI answers with citations and confidence boundaries.
- Admin content publishing workflow.

### Not in MVP
- Visa/legal decisions or guarantees.
- Direct university database integrations without formal permission.
- Account creation or collection of sensitive identity details.
- Payment, student verification, or document upload.

## Definition of done for prototype
1. User can start at Home and reach an essential guide.
2. User can complete a simple checklist task.
3. Every actionable fact is tied to a source or visibly marked as unverified placeholder.
4. No real personal information in screenshots, analytics, or test fixtures.

## Measurement ideas (to validate)
Task completion, time to find information, successful understanding, and qualitative feedback. No analytics are deployed or measured yet.
