# Research plan (not yet executed)

## Objective
Establish whether incoming international students have substantial, specific onboarding information problems and whether a digital guide could reduce those problems.

## Phase 1: desk research
Select one university in Kyrgyzstan and one in South Korea. Gather only publicly available official orientation pages, student handbooks, contact instructions, and international-office materials. Record the page title, URL, access date, language, and relevant section. Verify whether both universities offer comparable information before choosing them.

## Phase 2: comparison matrix
Use a consistent scoring rubric, defined before evaluating sources:

| Criterion | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Findability | How many steps are needed to reach essential information? |
| Clarity | Are instructions specific, current, and understandable? |
| Language accessibility | Are important instructions available in a usable language? |
| Completeness | Are onboarding steps, contacts, housing, and campus services covered? |
| Actionability | Can a student determine what to do next and whom to contact? |

For each observation, include the source and rationale. Do **not** assign scores without collecting evidence.

## Phase 3: optional voluntary interviews
Suggested non-leading questions:
1. What was most difficult during your first weeks?
2. Which university or local information did you need most?
3. Where did you look for answers, and what did not work?
4. What, if anything, was missing or confusing?
5. Can you show how you would find a particular piece of information using the existing resources?

Aim for a small, practical sample only if recruitment is possible. Record consent, use anonymous summaries, and store raw notes privately. Do not publish personal stories or recordings without explicit permission.

## Phase 4: synthesis and prototype validation
Group evidence into user needs, rank by frequency/severity where supported, sketch flows, and test comprehension of the clickable prototype. Report sample size, selection limitations, and unsupported assumptions.

## Deliverables
- Source inventory (URLs only, verified before release)
- Country comparison matrix
- Main findings and limitations
- Prototype and rationale
- Capstone presentation

**Current status:** No interviews, test results, or comparison scores have been documented in this repository.
