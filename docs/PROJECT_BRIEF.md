# Project brief (draft)

## Working title
UniBridge — Digital Assistant for International Students.

## Context
Three-month international capstone project exploring inclusive student experiences across Kyrgyzstan and South Korea. The proposed product is a **student support tool**, while the capstone research tests whether it addresses documented barriers.

## Target group
Incoming international students, especially those navigating their first days or weeks at a new institution. Start with a narrow, clearly defined target group at one institution before proposing wider availability.

## Problem hypothesis (unvalidated)
Students may find essential onboarding information fragmented, hard to locate, language-dependent, outdated, or difficult to understand.

## Primary research question
How clear, complete, discoverable, and accessible are orientation resources for incoming international students at a selected university in Kyrgyzstan compared with a selected university in South Korea?

## Proposed outcomes
1. Evidence-based comparison of official guidance using common evaluation criteria.
2. Prioritized list of information barriers and student needs.
3. UX wireframes and an interactive prototype for the most important student journeys.
4. If feasible, a small mobile MVP with curated information.
5. Final presentation describing methods, findings, limitations, and proposed solution.

## Success criteria (targets; not yet measured)
- At least two comparable sourcesets documented, one per country.
- A repeatable comparison matrix with 3–5 criteria.
- Student feedback collected only with consent, if possible.
- At least one end-to-end prototype journey that can be demonstrated.
- Findings and caveats clearly distinguished from assumptions.

## Out of scope for first MVP
Immigration/legal determinations, official university authorization, automatic form submission, payments, accommodation booking, emergencies dispatch, and advice represented as institutionally verified unless it truly is.
