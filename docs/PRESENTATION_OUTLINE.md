## Do not say

- "60 days without a visa", "Namba Taxi", "Papago / 1345 understand Kyrgyz", "112 works across Kyrgyzstan". All four are wrong ([research/FACT_CHECK_2026-11-23.md](research/FACT_CHECK_2026-11-23.md)).
- A number of Kyrgyz students in Korea without saying where it comes from (912 in Jan 2025 or about 3,000 per the ambassador in Nov 2026).
- "KWCAG certified". We can only say we self-checked.
- That interviews, surveys or usability tests happened. They have not.
- The May 2024 events only briefly and calmly, through the Oasis survey, as a reason for verified "where to get help" information.

## Prepared answers

- **"How are you better than Papago or Naver Map?"** We do not translate or route. We map apps and procedures from one country to the other.
- **"How will you keep it up to date?"** Every card has a check date and sources; we re-check once a semester.
- **"There are few Koreans in Bishkek. Who are the users?"** The two-way KR ↔ KG core first; the same structure can then serve English-speaking medical students in Kyrgyzstan, who were the majority of the 77% of medical students who were foreign in 2022.

## Evidence rule

Never state that interviews, comparisons or user tests happened unless records confirm this. Cite public materials accurately and do not publish information about participants without consent.
