# Competitors and existing services

*Desk research, 23 Nov 2026. Sources are linked inline and listed in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).*

## Short answer

We found **no product that maps everyday apps and procedures in both directions between Korea and Kyrgyzstan**. Official Korean services are split by ministry. Private "must-have apps" lists exist only in one direction (into Korea), are written for tourists, and contradict each other. On the Kyrgyz side, official portals cover admission and visas only.

## Korea: official services

| Service | What it covers | Languages | What it does not do | Sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Study in Korea** (NIIED, Ministry of Education) | Admission, scholarships. Relaunched 7 Mar 2025 with a 24-hour AI consultant and machine translation into about 100 languages | Human counselling in 6 languages including Russian | No mobile app; not about everyday life after arrival | [Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/study-in-korea-website-to-relaunch-with-enhanced-services/12282599); [Korea.net](https://www.korea.net/NewsFocus/Society/view?articleId=290867) |

## Korea: private lists and blogs

"Essential apps" lists repeat the same set: Naver Map, KakaoMap, Papago, KakaoTalk, Kakao T, Hanpass ([lacha.co.kr, 2026](https://lacha.co.kr/korea-guide/en/essential-apps-living-in-korea-2026/); [allo-korea](https://allo-korea.com/en/essential-apps-korea-2025/); [LG U+ Korea SIM](https://lguplus.com/korea-sim/eng/pc/support/blog/korea_travel_essential_apps_guide); [Klook](https://www.klook.com/blog/best-korea-apps-for-travel)). Mappings such as "Google Maps → Naver Map", "WhatsApp → KakaoTalk" and "Uber → Kakao T" appear only in blogs. They go one way, into Korea, are written for Western tourists, and disagree on whether Uber works and whether Naver Map has English ([annikim](https://annikim.github.io/nuff_blog/CATEGORIES/KOREA/koreaapps.html); [Airalo](https://www.airalo.com/blog/south-korea-travel-apps); [Culture Trip](https://theculturetrip.com/asia/south-korea/articles/12-essential-apps-for-expats-living-in-seoul)).

**Not treated as competitors:** "Klik", "Lingo Korea", "Kimchi Expat", "ImmiGo" and "Korea Life Guide" did not appear in any search result, so we do not name them as products. Kmong is a freelance marketplace, not an onboarding service.

## Kyrgyzstan

Only admission and visas are covered: the national portal educate.gov.kg ([Embassy of India advisory, 23 Jun 2026](https://www.indembbishkek.gov.in/docs/1782288341ADVISORY-23.06.2026.pdf)), EduGate with an English applicant guide ([AUCA](https://auca.kg/uploads/Admission%20Sample%20Exams/EduGate_Applicant_Guide_EN_26.pdf)), e-Visa ([KGMA](https://kgma.kg/en/inostrannym-studentam/viza-i-registratsiya-inostrannykh-studentov)) and accreditation of recruitment intermediaries ([24.kg](https://24.kg/en/article/kyrgyzstan-to-register-intermediaries-recruiting-foreign-students)). Search results are dominated by MBBS advertising sites ([Collegedunia](https://collegedunia.com/kyrgyzstan); [Careers360](https://studyabroad.careers360.com/articles/kyrgyzstan-mbbs-fees-2026)). We found no everyday-life guide, Telegram bot, or anything in Korean for students in Bishkek.

## How UniBridge Life Translator differs

| | KTO lists and blogs | Official portals | UniBridge Life Translator |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Direction | Into Korea only | Each portal covers its own country | **KR ↔ KG, both ways** |
| Audience | Tourists | Applicants, migrant families | Students in their first weeks |
| Structure | A list or an article | By ministry | **Task → pair of apps → pitfalls** |
| ARC, SMS and card caveats | Rarely | No | On the card |
| Freshness | Blogs contradict each other | Current within their own area | **Check date and sources on every card** |
| Languages | EN / JA / ZH | No Kyrgyz | EN / RU / KO |

**Positioning:** UniBridge is a connecting layer, not a replacement for government services. The "get an ARC" card sends you to HiKorea; "help in Russian" sends you to 1345 and Danuri. In one line: *KTO tells a tourist which Korean apps to download. UniBridge tells a student which app replaces the one they already use, in both directions.*
