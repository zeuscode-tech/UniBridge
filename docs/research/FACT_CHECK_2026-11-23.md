# Fact-check of Life Translator, 23 Nov 2026

*Desk research by the team. Each claim on a Life Translator card was checked against the sources linked below; the full source list is in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). Verdicts are as of 23 Nov 2026 and expire: re-check before reusing a fact.*

## Summary

- **Korea:** 18 claims checked. 5 confirmed, 8 partly true or outdated, 5 without a source yet. Two are **false**: Papago and the 1345 helpline do not support Kyrgyz.
- **Kyrgyzstan:** the most dangerous error was "60 visa-free days for Korean citizens". It is **false** (see below). "Namba Taxi" no longer exists under that name, and the single 112 emergency number is not confirmed nationwide.
- **More than ten facts changed in the twelve months before the check** (Baemin, KakaoBank, NHIS premium, visa-free stay, registration, bus payment, O! eSIM, WB Taxi). A static list goes out of date within a semester. This is why every card shows a check date.

## The "false friend" numbers

The same number means different things in the two countries. In Korea **119** is fire and ambulance and **112** is police ([NZ Embassy Seoul](https://www.mfat.govt.nz/en/countries-and-regions/asia/republic-of-korea-south/new-zealand-embassy/emergency-numbers-in-korea)). In Kyrgyzstan **119** is the migration call centre and the ambulance is **103** ([kaktus.media, 4 Sep 2024](https://kaktus.media/doc/508603_registraciia_inostrancev_grajdan_rossii_toje_po_novym_pravilam._otvety_na_glavnye_voprosy.html); [UN Kyrgyzstan](https://kyrgyzstan.un.org/en/node/35934)). A Korean student who dials 119 in Bishkek in an emergency reaches the migration service. The emergency card on the site says this first.

## Registration of foreigners in Kyrgyzstan: an open contradiction

Media reported that the stay without registration for citizens of Korea, the US, Japan and Germany was cut **from 60 to 30 days**, that re-registration is needed within **10 working days** after a change of address, and that 30+ days abroad cancels the registration ([economist.kg, 21 Sep 2026](https://economist.kg/pravo-znat/2026/09/21/v-kyrgyzstane-izmenili-poriadok-registratsii-inostrannykh-grazhdan-chto-nuzhno-znat/); [economist.kg, 28 Sep 2026](https://economist.kg/society/2026/09/28/minsotstruda-vnzh-registratsiya-inostrantsev.md)). On 21 Nov 2026 a deputy minister said the procedure "has not changed" for correctly registered foreigners ([24.kg](https://24.kg/en/article/389965)). We have not read the primary text of Resolution No. 600, so the site tells students to confirm with their university and the migration call centre 119.
