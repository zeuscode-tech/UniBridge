# Problem statement

*Desk research, 23 Nov 2026. No interviews or usability tests have been done yet. Every figure below carries its year and a source; the full list is in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).*

## Who

Newly arrived international students in their first weeks, moving in either direction between South Korea and Kyrgyzstan: Kyrgyz students arriving in Korea, and foreign students (including Korean students) arriving in Bishkek. The organisers' topic is "Cultural Inclusion for International Students": how clear orientation and everyday-life information is for newcomers.

## Barrier

The everyday tasks of the first weeks (taxi, transport card, bank, SIM, registration, insurance, emergency numbers) are described in scattered places, often not in a language the newcomer reads, and the rules change every few months. In Korea, the measured barrier is mainly **language and digital identity**. In Kyrgyzstan, it is mainly **safety, trust and changing rules**. Both point to the same gap: no verified, multilingual source for the first weeks.

## Evidence

**South Korea**

- **305,329** international students on study visas in Aug 2025 (Ministry of Justice), the first time above 300,000 ([Korea Times, 24 Dec 2025](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/southkorea/k-universities/20251224/vietnam-replaces-china-as-largest-source-of-intl-students-at-korean-universities)). The Ministry of Education counts fewer: **253,400** enrolled in universities in Apr 2025 ([Asia Economy, Sep 2025](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2025090310061899856)). The two series measure different things.
- **50.1%** of international students fall below the recommended language level (TOPIK 3 / TOEFL iBT 59), and **58.5%** outside Seoul (KCUE data; the publication year was not recorded in our notes: [Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/korean-universities-race-to-attract-international-students-but-fail-to-retain-them/12529877); [ICEF Monitor](https://monitor.icef.com/?p=48616)).
- Dropout at general universities rose from **4.7% (2019)** to **7.1% (2024)**. KEDI names poor adjustment, financial instability, and language and cultural barriers as causes ([Korea Times, 22 Jul 2025](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/amp/southkorea/education/20250722/do-korean-universities-really-understand-what-international-students-need)). The same article reports students missing information that was shared only in KakaoTalk group chats.
- Korea's main helpline 1345 works in 20 languages, but **not Kyrgyz** ([Korea Immigration Service](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration/1530/subview..do)). Papago does not support Kyrgyz either ([App Store, Papago](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1147874819?l=en-us)).
- Policy context: the 2026 **Study Korea: Talent Project** moves from student numbers to quality of support ([Asia News Network](https://asianews.network/south-korea-shifts-international-student-policy-from-numbers-to-quality/)).

**Kyrgyzstan**

- **Totals depend on the definition.** About **28,800** foreign students (Deputy Minister of Education, 20 May 2024, [Qazinform](https://qazinform.com/news/over-28000-foreign-students-study-at-universities-in-kyrgyzstan-3cb5b0)). Students from outside the CIS: **25,774** and **24,480** in the 2023 and 2024 columns of national statistics, academic years not stated ([stat.gov.kg](https://www.stat.gov.kg/en/opendata/category/5872/)). Older "all foreign students" figures of 71,700 and 68,700 use other definitions and dates ([24.kg](https://24.kg/english/267821_71700_foreign_students_study_at_universities_in_Kyrgyzstan_/amp); [Erasmus+](https://erasmus-networks.ec.europa.eu/book-page/country-page-kyrgyzstan)). We do not combine these series.
- Students from India and Pakistan: **17,585** in 2025/26, down 19% from 21,720 in 2024/25 ([Akchabar, Aug 2026](https://www.akchabar.kg/en/news/v-kirgizstane-chislo-studentov-iz-indii-i-pakistana-snizilos-na-4-tisyachi-mmiybvpjeydtovue)).
- **11,645** student visas issued in 2025, about a quarter of the previous year ([24.kg, 17 Oct 2025](https://24.kg/english/347565_Foreign_students_leaving_Kyrgyzstan_Number_of_visas_drops_fourfold)).
- **Safety and trust.** On the night of 17–18 May 2024, a mob attacked foreign-student hostels in Bishkek. Reported injury counts range from 14 to at least 29, and both governments denied reports of deaths ([RFE/RL via GlobalSecurity](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/library/news/2024/05/mil-240518-rferl03.htm); [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1834250); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Bishkek_riots)). In an Oasis Foundation survey presented in Oct 2025, **42%** of foreign students reported physical intimidation, **14%** sexual harassment or assault, and only **22%** turned to law enforcement when their rights were violated ([24.kg, 17 Oct 2025](https://24.kg/english/347565_Foreign_students_leaving_Kyrgyzstan_Number_of_visas_drops_fourfold)). The sample size was not published. The source does not say the riots caused the visa decline; we note only that the two coincide in time.
- **Changing rules, no multilingual line.** Stay and registration rules changed in Dec 2025, Mar 2026 and Sep 2026 ([kaktus.media, 31 Dec 2025](https://kaktus.media/doc/538103_v_kyrgyzstane_izmenen_poriadok_bezvizovogo_prebyvaniia_dlia_grajdan_55_stran.html); [economist.kg, 28 Mar 2026](https://economist.kg/society/2026/03/28/kyrghyzstan-izmienil-pravila-priebyvaniia-i-lieghalizatsii-dlia-inostrannykh-ghrazhdan/); [economist.kg, 21 Sep 2026](https://economist.kg/pravo-znat/2026/09/21/v-kyrgyzstane-izmenili-poriadok-registratsii-inostrannykh-grazhdan-chto-nuzhno-znat/)). We found no national multilingual helpline equivalent to Korea's 1345, and no official everyday-life guide for foreign students. Official portals cover admission and visas only ([Embassy of India advisory, 23 Jun 2026](https://www.indembbishkek.gov.in/docs/1782288341ADVISORY-23.06.2026.pdf); [EduGate guide, 2026](https://auca.kg/uploads/Admission%20Sample%20Exams/EduGate_Applicant_Guide_EN_26.pdf)).

**The KR–KG cohort.** Kyrgyz students in Korea: **912** in Jan 2025 ([Akchabar](https://www.akchabar.kg/en/news/bolee-20-tisyach-grazhdan-kirgizstana-obuchayutsya-za-granitsej-jgasvexjayehoghz)) versus "around 3,000" according to the Kyrgyz ambassador in Nov 2026 ([Korea Times, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/foreignaffairs/20261106/kyrgyzstan-seeks-to-turn-korea-central-asia-summit-pledges-into-reality)). The gap is probably definitional (degree students versus all study-visa holders). We use "roughly 1,000 to 3,000".

## Limitations

- This is desk research only. There are no interviews, surveys or usability tests by the team.
- Many Kyrgyz facts come from reputable local media quoting ministries (kaktus.media, 24.kg, economist.kg), not from primary government texts.
- Korean barriers to everyday tasks (ARC, bank, 본인인증, phone) are documented **qualitatively** in the press. We found no national survey with percentages, so we call them "frequently reported" and give no numbers.
- Rules change quickly. Every fact is dated 23 Nov 2026 and must be checked again before reuse.

## Not yet known

- The number of Korean students in Kyrgyzstan. No source gives it.
- An official per-country breakdown of Kyrgyz students in Korea. The [Study in Korea statistics page](https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr/ko/why/koreaStatistics.do) was not opened.
- Survey data on everyday barriers in Korea. The [KIHASA](https://www.kihasa.re.kr/api/kihasa/file/download?seq=19605) and [KCUE](https://www.kcue.or.kr/_upload/news/2024/05/02/_5e0f0c4f88747480e2a85c7c924d5456.pdf) reports and a [KoreaScience paper](https://koreascience.kr/article/JAKO202428443229204.pdf) were not opened.
- The Oasis survey's sample and method, and the contents of the [CESS 2025 paper](https://nomadit.co.uk/conference/cess2025/paper/91549).
- Whether Kyrgyzstan's Sept 2026 registration rules apply to students: the primary text of Resolution No. 600 was not retrieved, and a 21 Nov 2026 ministry statement appears to contradict media coverage ([24.kg](https://24.kg/en/article/389965)).
- What students themselves find hardest. Only interviews can answer this.
